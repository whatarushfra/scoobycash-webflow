🤔 QUESTIONS PEOPLE ACTUALLY ASK

Real talk, no fluff.

The honest answers to what people actually want to know — money, credit, banking, the works.

📊 CREDIT

I have bad credit. Will I actually get approved?

Probably yes. We don't run a credit check — not even a soft one. We look at your last 90 days of bank activity to confirm you've got steady income, and that's it. About 9 in 10 people who complete the application get approved.

If banks have turned you down before, that has zero bearing on your decision with us. Past denials, low scores, no credit history — we never see any of it.

💸 MONEY

What does this actually cost me?

Short-term loans cost more than bank loans — we won't pretend otherwise. Our regulated rate is $15 per $100 borrowed every 14 days, set under the Canadian Payday Loans Act, 2008.

Your full payback amount and bi-weekly installment breakdown are shown clearly on your contract before you sign anything. There are no hidden fees, no setup charges, and no early repayment penalties. The number you see at signing is the number you pay.

📊 CREDIT

Will applying hurt my credit score?

No. We don't pull your credit report or run any kind of check — hard or soft. Applying with ScoobyCash has zero impact on your credit score. You can apply, get approved, even sign the contract — none of it shows up on your credit file. The loan itself is not reported to the credit bureaus.

🔒 BANKING

What is Instant Banking Verification (IBV)?

IBV is how we verify your income without making you upload paystubs or bank statements. You log in directly to your bank's secure portal — we never see, store, or have access to your password.

In return, we get a one-time read-only copy of your last 90 days of statements, used only to confirm you have steady income. We can't move money. We can't change anything. We can't access your account again. The link expires automatically after 90 days.

It uses bank-grade 256-bit encryption — the same standard your bank uses for online banking. The technology is widely used across Canadian fintech, including tax filing and budgeting apps.

📅 REPAY

What if I can't repay on time?

Tell us before the payment is due, not after. We can defer most installments by up to 14 days at no extra cost. We'd rather work with you than chase you.

A missed payment without notice does incur an NSF fee from your bank plus a late fee from us. We don't sell debts to collectors and we don't garnish wages. We're a Canadian-licensed lender, not a loan shark.

📅 REPAY

Are all payments eligible for a deferral?

Most installments can be deferred by up to 14 days at no extra cost — just let us know before the payment is due. The first and last payments of your loan can't be deferred, and we can't defer two consecutive payments.

🔒 BANKING

Who can't get a loan with ScoobyCash?

Honest answer: our model needs predictable income from steady employment to approve a loan responsibly. We can't lend to applicants whose primary income is government assistance (ODSP, EI), disability benefits, CPP, child tax, or private pensions.

We also can't lend to people who are self-employed, on student loans, currently filing for bankruptcy or about to enter a consumer proposal, or under 18. If that's you, we'd rather tell you upfront than waste your time.

💸 MONEY

How much can I borrow on my first loan?

First-time borrowers can be approved for $250 to $1,500. Your specific limit depends on your verified income — we won't lend you more than you can comfortably repay across your bi-weekly installments.

Returning customers in good standing often qualify for higher amounts on subsequent loans.

🔒 BANKING

Is ScoobyCash a real, licensed company?

Yes. ScoobyCash is a direct lender (not a broker), provincially licensed in Ontario, BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador. We're regulated under the Canadian Payday Loans Act, 2008. Quebec residents are not currently eligible.
